This Privacy Policy explains how HR07 Opportunity Desk (“the App”) handles information when staff use it to operate our Threads coordination workflows for Vietnam medical-aesthetics and travel assistance related to HR07 BEAUTY CLINIC.

1. Who we are

The App is an internal operations tool for authorized HR07 staff. It is not a consumer social network. The public-facing Threads account used with the App is @khuu7681.

2. Information we process

  • Account credentials / tokens: Meta/Threads access tokens and account identifiers needed to call Meta Graph / Threads APIs on behalf of the authorized account.
  • Public Threads content: public posts, replies, media IDs, permalinks, and related public metadata returned by Meta APIs (including keyword search and mentions once permitted).
  • Operator actions: draft replies, approval decisions, publish timestamps, and basic delivery status needed for audit and quality control.
  • Insights: aggregate or post-level metrics Meta provides for content the authorized account may access.

We do not intentionally collect sensitive personal data categories beyond what appears in public Threads content or what Meta returns for the authorized account.

3. How we use information

  • To discover public conversations relevant to Vietnam clinic / travel coordination.
  • To draft helpful replies for human review.
  • To publish only after an operator explicitly approves.
  • To measure reply/post performance and improve operations.
  • To secure the App, prevent abuse, and comply with Meta Platform Terms.

We do not sell personal data. We do not use Platform data to train unrelated public AI models.

4. Human approval gate

Outbound replies and posts from the App require human confirmation. Automated discovery may draft suggestions; publishing is not automatic.

5. Sharing

We do not sell or rent personal data. We share data only with:

  • Meta Platforms: as required to use Threads/Graph APIs.
  • Service providers: only if needed to host or operate the App, under confidentiality and purpose limits. Current review configuration states no separate external processors beyond Meta for the Threads workflow described here; update this page if vendors change.
  • Legal requirements: when required by applicable law.

6. Retention

Operational logs, hit records, drafts, and publish history are retained only as long as needed for clinic operations, quality review, dispute handling, and Meta compliance, then deleted or anonymized.

7. Security

Access tokens are stored in restricted environment configuration, not pasted into public chats. Access is limited to authorized operators.

8. Your choices and deletion

Threads users may manage content through Threads/Meta settings. To request deletion of App-stored operational copies related to you, see our Data Deletion Instructions.

9. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in the relevant jurisdiction).

10. Changes

We may update this Policy. The “Last updated” date will change when we do.

11. Contact

Privacy questions: contact the App admin listed in Meta Developer settings for HR07 Opportunity Desk, or clinic operations for HR07 BEAUTY CLINIC. Legal entity: 羣富企業社, Taiwan (統編 60187692).
