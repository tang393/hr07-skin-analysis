What we store

The App may store Meta/Threads tokens for the authorized operator account, public post identifiers/permalinks discovered during monitoring, draft replies, approval records, and publish/delivery logs.

How to request deletion

  1. Email the App administrator listed in the Meta Developer App Dashboard for HR07 Opportunity Desk, or contact HR07 BEAUTY CLINIC operations, with subject line “HR07 Opportunity Desk data deletion”.
  2. Include: your name or company contact, Threads username (if applicable), approximate date range, and whether you want (a) App-stored operational copies deleted, and/or (b) help removing content you posted via the authorized account.
  3. We will verify the request and delete App-stored personal/operational data that is not required to be retained for legal or Meta Platform compliance, typically within 30 days.

What Meta controls

Content that already exists on Threads remains subject to Meta/Threads account controls. Users can delete their own Threads posts/replies in the Threads app. Revoking the App’s access from Meta/Threads settings will stop new API access; contact us to also purge local App copies.

Operator / staff offboarding

When a staff operator leaves, we revoke their App access and rotate tokens as needed.
